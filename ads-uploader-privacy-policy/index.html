Ads Uploader Privacy Policy

Effective October 13, 2026

This policy describes how Alloy Media ("Alloy Media", "we", "us") handles information in connection with the Alloy Ads Uploader, an internal tool that Alloy Media uses to create advertisements in Meta (Facebook and Instagram) ad accounts that Alloy Media manages for itself and on behalf of its clients.

Who uses the tool

The Ads Uploader is operated only by Alloy Media staff. It is not offered to the public, and it does not provide a login for, or collect information from, members of the public or Facebook users.

Information we access

  • Ad account information from Meta's Marketing API, for ad accounts that have been explicitly shared with Alloy Media: campaign, ad set, ad and creative settings, such as names, destination links, copy and tracking configuration.
  • Page information needed to publish ads under a Facebook Page or Instagram account that the account owner has authorized us to advertise for.
  • Creative files and ad copy (images, videos and text) supplied by Alloy Media or its clients for upload as advertisements.

The tool does not access personal profiles, messages, friends lists or the personal data of people who see or interact with ads.

How we use it

We use this information solely to create and manage advertisements in the ad accounts we are authorized to manage. We do not sell it, use it to build profiles of individuals, or use it for any purpose unrelated to managing those ad accounts.

Sharing

Information is shared only with Meta, as required to create advertisements, and with the client that owns the relevant ad account. We may disclose information if required by law.

Storage and retention

Access credentials are stored securely and are never published. Creative files and upload records are kept only as long as needed to create and review the advertisements, then deleted. Advertisements themselves are stored by Meta under Meta's own policies.

Deleting your data

If you are an ad account or Page owner and would like us to stop accessing your account or delete any related records we hold, email alex@alloymedia.io. You can also remove Alloy Media's access at any time in your Meta Business settings.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it last changed.
